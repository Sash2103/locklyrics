# LockLyrics

A Spotify player with synced lyrics on your Mac's lock screen. Tap the album art for a full-screen view with the lyrics beside it.

This page holds only the finished app. New versions are published here, and every installed copy updates itself.

## Download

**[LockLyrics.zip](https://github.com/Sash2103/locklyrics/releases/latest/download/LockLyrics.zip)**

You need a MacBook with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later (it draws on the built-in screen), and the Spotify desktop app.

## Install

1. Unzip it and move **LockLyrics** to your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS won't open it the first time, since it isn't from the App Store: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**.
3. When asked, allow LockLyrics to control Spotify.
4. In LockLyrics' Settings (its icon in the menu bar), click **Sign in to Spotify…** for lyrics, or switch on LRCLIB.
5. Play a song and lock your Mac (⌃⌘Q).

## Updates

LockLyrics checks this page every few hours and installs new versions by itself while your Mac is unlocked, keeping your settings and sign-in. **Settings → Updates → Check Now** checks right away.
